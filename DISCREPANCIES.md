| 3 | Table 1 "enlargements" / "squarefree enlargements" (ℤ[√2]) | see report.md, gate A.3.3 table | 1489/7181/3653/38871/13688 and 130/341/233/1096/581 | **Open, explained but not reproduced item by item.** Our enlargements are all lattices M with ⟨roots⟩ ⊆ M ⊆ H, where H = {v : 2 r·v / r² ∈ O for every configuration root r} and every configuration root is primitive in M. By construction this is the complete set of lattices in which the configuration vectors are roots, so it is a superset of what is needed. Mark's counts are smaller for every type. Her §3.5 says that for pairs, "reflection-stable enlargements of a subset of the roots were discarded when the remaining roots were not primitive", and §2.9 defines the hull over all roots of R. Neither variant reproduces her numbers here: the all-roots-of-R hull for type (15), roots of R in the coefficient box B, gives 1266/129 (B=1), 1093/128 (B=2), 996/124 (B=3) against her 1489/130. The all-roots hull is *not* a valid completeness argument (a root of R need not be a root of an enlargement M), so it can drop lattices. Whether Mark's procedure lost any reflective lattice is decided at gate A.3.4, by comparing our final reflective list with her 432. The order of her local-simplicity removal relative to the SSF column is also ambiguous (see OPEN_QUESTIONS Q3). |
